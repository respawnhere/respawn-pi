# NIST SP 800-53 Rev. 5 — Security and Privacy Controls — requirements checklist
> Source: https://nvlpubs.nist.gov/nistpubs/SpecialPublications/NIST.SP.800-53r5.pdf + catalog https://csrc.nist.gov/pubs/sp/800/53/r5/upd1/final · NIST Special Publication 800-53, Revision 5 ("Security and Privacy Controls for Information Systems and Organizations"), published Sept 2020, Update 1 effective 10 Dec 2020; baselines in the companion SP 800-53B; a minor patch release (5.2.0, 27 Aug 2025) adds/revises controls. Catalog of ~1,000+ base controls across 20 families. · retrieved 2026-06-22 · U.S. Government work — public domain (NIST). Reference only — not legal advice. Maps to the unified primitives in docs/reference/compliance-requirements.md.

## Applies when
- **800-53 is a control catalog, not a law.** For a private SaaS on managed infra it is a **lookup reference** — the master vocabulary of security/privacy controls that nearly every other framework (SOC 2, ISO 27001, HIPAA Security Rule, NIST CSF/Privacy Framework, PCI) maps back to. You build once and cross-walk to many.
- **Direct scope is U.S. federal:** 800-53 is mandatory for federal information systems via FISMA, and is the catalog **FedRAMP** draws from to authorize cloud services sold to the U.S. government. If you ever pursue FedRAMP (or sell to a federal agency, or a defense/CUI contract triggering NIST SP 800-171, which derives from 800-53), this becomes your live target — typically at the **Moderate** baseline, and that path requires a **3PAO assessment and an agency/JAB authorization (ATO)**, not self-attestation.
- **Baselines (SP 800-53B):** controls are selected into **Low / Moderate / High** baselines by system impact level (FIPS 199 confidentiality/integrity/availability categorization), plus a separate **Privacy** baseline applied regardless of impact level. FedRAMP and agency programs publish their own **profiles/overlays** that tailor (add, remove, or parameterize) the baseline for a context. You do not implement all ~1,000 controls — you implement the baseline your impact level selects, then tailor.
- **Inheritance on managed infra:** physical/environmental (PE) and maintenance (MA) controls, plus large parts of SC/CP, are **inherited** from the cloud provider (Supabase / Fly / Cloudflare / Vercel / underlying Postgres host). In FedRAMP terms these are provider-responsible or **shared**; you document them via the provider's own FedRAMP/SOC 2 package and your **vendor register**, and you own the **customer-responsibility** controls (configuration, access, app-layer crypto, logging).
- **Not a privacy law substitute:** the PT family supports privacy programs but a lawyer / DPO still owns DPIA/PIA, lawful-basis, and notice decisions.
- This file enumerates **by family** (20 families, two-letter IDs) with flagship controls — it is a summary map, not a reproduction of the full catalog.

## Requirements
### AC — Access Control (AC-1 … AC-25)
- **Requires:** Manage accounts and enforce authorized access to systems and information: account management, access enforcement, separation of duties, least privilege, session/logon controls, remote-access and wireless restrictions. Flagship: **AC-2 Account Management**, **AC-3 Access Enforcement**, **AC-6 Least Privilege**, **AC-7 Unsuccessful Logon Attempts**, **AC-17 Remote Access**.
- **In code:** Postgres **RLS on every table** (AC-3), application **RBAC** with deny-by-default and least-privilege scoped service tokens (AC-6), automated provisioning/deprovisioning tied to the roles table (AC-2), lockout on failed logins (AC-7), and admin consoles reachable only over authenticated, restricted paths (AC-17). Same access primitive satisfies SOC 2 CC6, ISO 27001 A.5/A.8, HIPAA §164.312(a).
- **Primitive:** 5. Access control — RLS/RBAC/MFA/least-privilege
- **Cite:** SP 800-53 Rev. 5, AC family (AC-2, AC-3, AC-6, AC-7, AC-17)

### AT — Awareness and Training (AT-1 … AT-6)
- **Requires:** Provide security/privacy **literacy training and awareness** to all users (AT-2) and **role-based training** to those with significant security/privacy responsibilities (AT-3); retain training records (AT-4).
- **In code:** Not code — a process control. Track completion in an onboarding checklist / HR system with timestamps; require annual security-awareness and secure-development training for anyone with prod or data access; retain dated completion records as evidence.
- **Primitive:** 5. Access control — RLS/RBAC/MFA/least-privilege (competence/accountability support)
- **Cite:** SP 800-53 Rev. 5, AT family (AT-2, AT-3, AT-4)

### AU — Audit and Accountability (AU-1 … AU-16)
- **Requires:** Generate, protect, review, and retain audit records sufficient to reconstruct events and hold users accountable: **AU-2 Event Logging**, **AU-3 Content of Audit Records**, **AU-6 Audit Record Review, Analysis, and Reporting**, **AU-9 Protection of Audit Information**, **AU-11 Audit Record Retention**.
- **In code:** Centralized, append-only **immutable audit log** (app + auth + infra) shipped off-box; capture who/what/when/where/outcome (AU-3); restrict and integrity-protect the logs themselves (AU-9); automated review/alerting on anomalies (AU-6); retention per the program's defined period (AU-11). This is the AU evidence base SOC 2 CC4/CC7 and HIPAA §164.312(b) also rely on.
- **Primitive:** 6. Immutable audit logging
- **Cite:** SP 800-53 Rev. 5, AU family (AU-2, AU-3, AU-6, AU-9, AU-11)

### CA — Assessment, Authorization, and Monitoring (CA-1 … CA-9)
- **Requires:** Assess controls (**CA-2 Control Assessments**), track and remediate weaknesses (**CA-5 Plan of Action and Milestones / POA&M**), formally **authorize** the system to operate (**CA-6 Authorization / ATO**), and run **continuous monitoring** (**CA-7**).
- **In code:** Stand up continuous control monitoring — scheduled checks asserting MFA-on-everyone, no public buckets, RLS on every table, backups succeeding, certs valid — that auto-open tickets on failure; maintain a tracked **POA&M** queue with SLAs (CA-5); run platform advisors (e.g. Supabase security advisor) and CI scanners as recurring assessments (CA-2/CA-7). The ATO (CA-6) is a management sign-off, formalized under FedRAMP.
- **Primitive:** 6. Immutable audit logging (continuous monitoring + evidence)
- **Cite:** SP 800-53 Rev. 5, CA family (CA-2, CA-5, CA-6, CA-7)

### CM — Configuration Management (CM-1 … CM-14)
- **Requires:** Establish and enforce secure baseline configurations and control changes to them: **CM-2 Baseline Configuration**, **CM-3 Configuration Change Control**, **CM-6 Configuration Settings**, **CM-8 System Component Inventory**.
- **In code:** Infrastructure-as-code so config is a reviewable, versioned **baseline** (CM-2); all changes via PR with required review + CI status checks, versioned reviewed migrations, separate staging/prod promotion (CM-3); hardened secure settings enforced as policy-as-code with drift detection (CM-6); an auto-generated **component inventory** of services, images, and dependencies (CM-8). Overlaps SOC 2 CC8 change management and ISO 27001 A.8.9/A.8.32.
- **Primitive:** 6. Immutable audit logging (change/deploy trail) + 4. Data inventory + sensitive-field tagging (CM-8 component inventory)
- **Cite:** SP 800-53 Rev. 5, CM family (CM-2, CM-3, CM-6, CM-8)

### CP — Contingency Planning (CP-1 … CP-13)
- **Requires:** Plan for and recover from disruption: **CP-2 Contingency Plan**, **CP-9 System Backup**, **CP-10 System Recovery and Reconstitution** (with defined RTO/RPO).
- **In code:** Automated backups (Supabase PITR / scheduled dumps) with off-region copies (CP-9); documented and **tested restore drills** with results (CP-10); a written contingency/BC-DR plan with roles and RTO/RPO targets (CP-2). Most of the resilience substrate (region failover, durable storage) is **inherited** from the managed platform — document it via the vendor register. Mirrors SOC 2 A1 availability.
- **Primitive:** 7. Retention + deletion jobs (backup/restore lifecycle) + 8. Incident-response + breach pipeline (recovery)
- **Cite:** SP 800-53 Rev. 5, CP family (CP-2, CP-9, CP-10)

### IA — Identification and Authentication (IA-1 … IA-12)
- **Requires:** Uniquely identify and authenticate users and devices: **IA-2 Identification and Authentication (Organizational Users)** — its enhancements mandate **multi-factor authentication** for privileged and network access; **IA-5 Authenticator Management** (password/secret/key policy); **IA-8 Identification and Authentication (Non-organizational Users)**. Authenticator strength per NIST SP 800-63.
- **In code:** **MFA mandatory** on all human accounts and cloud consoles via Supabase Auth (IA-2 + enhancements); strong authenticator/secret management — rotation, no shared creds, secrets in a managed store (IA-5); separate identity handling for external/customer users (IA-8). Same MFA primitive satisfies SOC 2 CC6.1, PCI Req. 8, HIPAA §164.312(d).
- **Primitive:** 5. Access control — RLS/RBAC/MFA/least-privilege
- **Cite:** SP 800-53 Rev. 5, IA family (IA-2, IA-5, IA-8)

### IR — Incident Response (IR-1 … IR-9)
- **Requires:** Detect, handle, report, and recover from incidents: **IR-4 Incident Handling**, **IR-6 Incident Reporting** (including timelines to authorities/affected parties), **IR-8 Incident Response Plan**.
- **In code:** A written **incident-response runbook** with severities, on-call, and timelines (IR-8); a breach-handling pipeline — triage → contain → eradicate → recover → notify — wired to comms templates and the audit log (IR-4); reporting paths to required recipients with dated records (IR-6). Same pipeline serves GDPR Art. 33/34, HIPAA Breach Notification, SOC 2 CC7.
- **Primitive:** 8. Incident-response + breach pipeline
- **Cite:** SP 800-53 Rev. 5, IR family (IR-4, IR-6, IR-8)

### MA — Maintenance (MA-1 … MA-7)
- **Requires:** Control system maintenance and the tools/personnel performing it: **MA-2 Controlled Maintenance**, **MA-4 Nonlocal Maintenance** (remote maintenance over secured, authenticated channels), **MA-5 Maintenance Personnel**.
- **In code:** On managed infra the physical/hardware maintenance is **inherited** from the provider — cover via their FedRAMP/SOC 2 report in the vendor register. Your responsibility: authenticated, logged, least-privilege access for any remote administration (MA-4) and authorized, attributable operators (MA-5).
- **Primitive:** 5. Access control — RLS/RBAC/MFA/least-privilege + 9. Vendor / sub-processor register (inherited hardware maintenance)
- **Cite:** SP 800-53 Rev. 5, MA family (MA-2, MA-4, MA-5)

### MP — Media Protection (MP-1 … MP-8)
- **Requires:** Protect digital/physical media across its life cycle: **MP-2 Media Access**, **MP-4 Media Storage**, **MP-6 Media Sanitization** (sanitize/destroy before reuse or disposal), **MP-7 Media Use** (restrict removable media).
- **In code:** Physical-media custody is **inherited** from the cloud provider. Your responsibility maps to the data layer: encryption-at-rest so storage is unreadable if media is lost (ties to SC-28); **crypto-erase / verified deletion** of decommissioned volumes, buckets, and backups in the retention pipeline (MP-6); restrict and log access to data exports/removable copies (MP-2/MP-7). Overlaps SOC 2 CC6.5 secure disposal.
- **Primitive:** 7. Retention + deletion jobs (sanitization) + 1. Encryption (media-at-rest protection)
- **Cite:** SP 800-53 Rev. 5, MP family (MP-2, MP-4, MP-6, MP-7)

### PE — Physical and Environmental Protection (PE-1 … PE-23)
- **Requires:** Restrict and monitor physical access to facilities and protect against environmental hazards: **PE-2 Physical Access Authorizations**, **PE-3 Physical Access Control**, **PE-6 Monitoring Physical Access**.
- **In code:** Almost entirely **inherited** — for a SaaS on Supabase/Fly/Cloudflare/Vercel there is no facility you operate. Satisfy by recording the provider's data-center physical/environmental controls (from their FedRAMP/SOC 2/ISO 27001 reports) in the **vendor register**; flag any local devices with prod access for endpoint controls.
- **Primitive:** 9. Vendor / sub-processor register (inherited physical controls)
- **Cite:** SP 800-53 Rev. 5, PE family (PE-2, PE-3, PE-6)

### PL — Planning (PL-1 … PL-11)
- **Requires:** Develop and maintain system security/privacy plans and architecture: **PL-2 System Security and Privacy Plans (SSP)**, **PL-4 Rules of Behavior**, **PL-8 Security and Privacy Architectures**.
- **In code:** Maintain a versioned **System Security Plan** documenting boundary, components, data flows, and control implementation (PL-2) — under FedRAMP this is the central authorization artifact; record acceptable-use **rules of behavior** with signed, timestamped acknowledgment (PL-4); document the security/privacy **architecture** (PL-8). Keep these in-repo as diffable markdown driven off the data inventory.
- **Primitive:** 4. Data inventory + sensitive-field tagging (SSP boundary/data-flow basis)
- **Cite:** SP 800-53 Rev. 5, PL family (PL-2, PL-4, PL-8)

### PM — Program Management (PM-1 … PM-32)
- **Requires:** Organization-wide (not system-specific) program controls: **PM-5 System Inventory**, **PM-9 Risk Management Strategy**, **PM-11 Mission and Business Process Definition**, **PM-31 Continuous Monitoring Strategy**. PM controls are **independent of impact baseline** — expected of every organization.
- **In code:** An org-level **system/asset inventory** (PM-5); a documented risk-management strategy and risk register reviewed at least annually (PM-9); a defined continuous-monitoring strategy that the CA-7 automation implements (PM-31). For a solo/small team these are lightweight living docs, but they must exist and be dated.
- **Primitive:** 4. Data inventory + sensitive-field tagging (PM-5) + 8. Incident-response + breach pipeline (program-level risk)
- **Cite:** SP 800-53 Rev. 5, PM family (PM-5, PM-9, PM-11, PM-31)

### PS — Personnel Security (PS-1 … PS-9)
- **Requires:** Manage risk from people with system access across their life cycle: **PS-3 Personnel Screening**, **PS-4 Personnel Termination** (timely access revocation on departure), **PS-7 External Personnel Security** (contractors/third parties).
- **In code:** Screening per role sensitivity (PS-3); **immediate, automated deprovisioning** on offboarding tied to the roles table (PS-4) — same mechanism as AC-2; flow-down security requirements to contractors and capture them in the vendor register (PS-7).
- **Primitive:** 5. Access control — RLS/RBAC/MFA/least-privilege (lifecycle) + 9. Vendor / sub-processor register (external personnel)
- **Cite:** SP 800-53 Rev. 5, PS family (PS-3, PS-4, PS-7)

### PT — PII Processing and Transparency (PT-1 … PT-8)
- **Requires:** Privacy controls for processing personally identifiable information: **PT-2 Authority to Process PII** (lawful basis), **PT-3 PII Processing Purposes** (purpose limitation), **PT-4 Consent**, **PT-5 Privacy Notice**, **PT-6 System of Records Notice** (federal-specific). New family in Rev. 5; supports the separate Privacy control baseline.
- **In code:** A **consent + preference store** (granular, timestamped, withdrawable, GPC-aware) gating collection (PT-4); a versioned in-product **privacy notice** (PT-5); purpose tags in the **data inventory** so processing stays within stated purposes (PT-3); lawful-basis documentation (PT-2) — a lawyer/DPO owns the legal determination. Maps to GDPR Art. 6/13–14, CCPA notice/opt-out, and the NIST Privacy Framework.
- **Primitive:** 3. Consent + preference store (PT-4) + 4. Data inventory + sensitive-field tagging (PT-2/PT-3) + 2. DSAR engine (transparency/access support)
- **Cite:** SP 800-53 Rev. 5, PT family (PT-2, PT-3, PT-4, PT-5, PT-6)

### RA — Risk Assessment (RA-1 … RA-10)
- **Requires:** Categorize systems and assess risk on an ongoing basis: **RA-3 Risk Assessment**, **RA-5 Vulnerability Monitoring and Scanning**, **RA-7 Risk Response**, **RA-9 Criticality Analysis**. (System categorization per FIPS 199 / RA-2 drives baseline selection.)
- **In code:** Recurring **vulnerability scanning** of dependencies, images, and infra wired into CI with results tracked to remediation (RA-5) — overlaps SI-2; a maintained risk register with treatment decisions (RA-3/RA-7) driven off the data inventory; criticality analysis of components feeding supply-chain (SR) decisions (RA-9). Same scanning evidence serves SOC 2 CC7.1 and PCI Req. 11.
- **Primitive:** 6. Immutable audit logging (scan/finding evidence) + 4. Data inventory + sensitive-field tagging (risk basis)
- **Cite:** SP 800-53 Rev. 5, RA family (RA-3, RA-5, RA-7, RA-9)

### SA — System and Services Acquisition (SA-1 … SA-23)
- **Requires:** Build security into acquisition and the SDLC: **SA-4 Acquisition Process** (security requirements in contracts), **SA-8 Security and Privacy Engineering Principles**, **SA-11 Developer Testing and Evaluation**, **SA-15 Development Process, Standards, and Tools**, **SA-22 Unsupported System Components** (no end-of-life software).
- **In code:** Secure-by-design/-default engineering principles (SA-8); security/privacy requirements written into vendor contracts and DPAs (SA-4); developer security testing — SAST, dependency tests, code review — gating merge in CI (SA-11/SA-15); dependency-currency checks and alerts to retire **unsupported/EOL** components (SA-22). Couples tightly with SR (supply chain) and CM (change control).
- **Primitive:** 9. Vendor / sub-processor register (SA-4 acquisition) + 6. Immutable audit logging (SDLC/test evidence)
- **Cite:** SP 800-53 Rev. 5, SA family (SA-4, SA-8, SA-11, SA-15, SA-22)

### SC — System and Communications Protection (SC-1 … SC-51)
- **Requires:** Protect information in transit and at rest and at system boundaries: **SC-7 Boundary Protection**, **SC-8 Transmission Confidentiality and Integrity**, **SC-12 Cryptographic Key Establishment and Management**, **SC-13 Cryptographic Protection**, **SC-28 Protection of Information at Rest**. (FedRAMP requires FIPS 140-validated cryptography.)
- **In code:** **TLS 1.2+ everywhere** with HSTS at the Cloudflare/Fly/Vercel edge (SC-8); **AES-256 at rest** for Postgres + storage, sensitive columns encrypted (SC-28); approved algorithms only and keys held in a managed secret store / KMS with rotation, never in the repo (SC-12/SC-13); WAF + rate limiting + private DB networking and deny-by-default ingress for **boundary protection** (SC-7). This is the same crypto control as HIPAA §164.312(e), GDPR Art. 32, PCI Req. 4 — build once.
- **Primitive:** 1. Encryption (TLS 1.2+ in transit, AES-256 at rest, key management)
- **Cite:** SP 800-53 Rev. 5, SC family (SC-7, SC-8, SC-12, SC-13, SC-28)

### SI — System and Information Integrity (SI-1 … SI-23)
- **Requires:** Maintain integrity by remediating flaws and detecting threats: **SI-2 Flaw Remediation** (timely patching), **SI-3 Malicious Code Protection**, **SI-4 System Monitoring**, **SI-7 Software, Firmware, and Information Integrity**.
- **In code:** Automated dependency/patch updates with SLAs for critical fixes (SI-2) — ties to RA-5 scanning; malware/anomaly protection at edge and runtime (SI-3); centralized **system monitoring** and alerting on suspicious behavior (SI-4); integrity verification of artifacts and configs, e.g. signed builds and checksums (SI-7). SI-2 flaw remediation is the patch-cadence control SOC 2 CC7.1 and PCI Req. 6 also test.
- **Primitive:** 8. Incident-response + breach pipeline (detection/response) + 6. Immutable audit logging (monitoring)
- **Cite:** SP 800-53 Rev. 5, SI family (SI-2, SI-3, SI-4, SI-7)

### SR — Supply Chain Risk Management (SR-1 … SR-12)
- **Requires:** Manage risk across the ICT supply chain: **SR-3 Supply Chain Controls and Processes**, **SR-4 Provenance** (track component origin/integrity), **SR-5 Acquisition Strategies, Tools, and Methods**, **SR-11 Component Authenticity**. New family in Rev. 5; aligns with NIST SP 800-161.
- **In code:** Generate and retain an **SBOM** for releases and track component **provenance** (SR-4) — the SBOM step in `templates/ci/security.yml` (alongside gitleaks secret-scanning and dependency-audit); verify dependency integrity / signatures to catch tampered or counterfeit components (SR-11); document supply-chain processes and trusted acquisition channels (SR-3/SR-5). The SBOM control is shared with EU CRA and NIS2 supply-chain obligations — emit once, map to all.
- **Primitive:** 9. Vendor / sub-processor register (SR-3/SR-5) + 6. Immutable audit logging (SBOM/provenance evidence)
- **Cite:** SP 800-53 Rev. 5, SR family (SR-3, SR-4, SR-5, SR-11)

## Evidence to retain
- **Baseline & tailoring:** FIPS 199 system categorization, the selected baseline (Low/Moderate/High per SP 800-53B), and the tailoring/overlay record showing which controls were added, removed, or parameterized (and FedRAMP profile if applicable).
- **System Security Plan (PL-2):** current SSP describing boundary, components, data flows, and the implementation status of each in-scope control — the central authorization artifact; updated on significant change.
- **Assessment & authorization (CA):** control-assessment results / SAR, the **POA&M** (CA-5) with open weaknesses, remediation owners, and dates, and the authorization (ATO) decision (CA-6).
- **Access (AC/IA):** MFA-enforcement proof, RBAC/RLS configuration, provisioning/deprovisioning records, periodic access reviews, privileged-user list.
- **Crypto (SC/MP):** TLS/cert inventory, at-rest encryption settings, key-management and rotation evidence, sanitization/crypto-erase logs for decommissioned media.
- **Audit & monitoring (AU/SI/RA/CA):** immutable logs covering the assessment window, alert history, vulnerability-scan results (RA-5/SI-2), continuous-monitoring outputs.
- **Change & config (CM):** baseline-config definitions, PR/review and CI history, component inventory (CM-8), drift-detection results.
- **Contingency (CP):** backup success logs and **restore-test** results with RTO/RPO.
- **Incident (IR):** incident-response plan plus every dated incident record (detection → reporting → recovery).
- **Training & personnel (AT/PS):** dated training-completion records, screening and timely-termination/deprovisioning evidence.
- **Privacy (PT):** privacy-notice versions, consent/preference records, lawful-basis and purpose documentation, and any PIA/DPIA (lawyer/DPO sign-off).
- **Supply chain (SR/SA):** SBOMs, provenance/authenticity verification records, secret-scan and dependency-audit reports from `templates/ci/security.yml`, vendor security/contract requirements.
- **Inherited controls:** the cloud providers' FedRAMP/SOC 2/ISO 27001 packages on file in the vendor register, with the customer-vs-provider responsibility split documented for PE/MA/CP/SC inheritance.
