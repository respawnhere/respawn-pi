# NIST SP 800-171 Revision 3 — Protecting Controlled Unclassified Information (CUI) — requirements checklist
> Source: https://csrc.nist.gov/pubs/sp/800/171/r3/final · https://nvlpubs.nist.gov/nistpubs/SpecialPublications/NIST.SP.800-171r3.pdf · NIST SP 800-171 Rev. 3 (final, May 14, 2024; assessment companion NIST SP 800-171A Rev. 3) · retrieved 2026-06-22 · US Government work, public domain (no rights reserved). Reference only — not legal advice. Maps to the unified primitives in docs/reference/compliance-requirements.md.

## Applies when
- You handle **Controlled Unclassified Information (CUI)** in a nonfederal system — i.e. you pursue or hold US Government / DoD contracts or subcontracts, or you are in the supply chain of a prime that flows the obligation down. A solo SaaS on managed infra is in scope **only** if it processes, stores, or transmits CUI on behalf of a federal agency or a contractor; ordinary commercial SaaS is out of scope.
- Imposed contractually, not by statute: DFARS clause **252.204-7012** (Safeguarding Covered Defense Information and Cyber Incident Reporting) requires "adequate security" per SP 800-171, plus 72-hour cyber-incident reporting to DoD; DFARS **252.204-7019/-7020** require a SPRS self-assessment score; **CMMC 2.0 / 32 CFR Part 170** layers third-party certification (Level 2 ≈ full SP 800-171) on top.
- **Effective-date caveat (verify against your contract):** DFARS 7012 and CMMC (32 CFR Part 170) still reference **Rev. 2** (110 requirements) as of mid-2026. **Rev. 3** is final but not yet incorporated into DFARS/CMMC; DoD's memorandum of **April 15, 2025** assigns values for all **88 Organization-Defined Parameters (ODPs)** in Rev. 3, in preparation. Confirm which revision your specific contract names before scoping — do not assume Rev. 3 applies yet.
- Rev. 3 structure (this checklist): **17 families, 03.01–03.17**; **97 security requirements** (down from Rev. 2's 110); **NIST SP 800-53 Rev. 5 is the single authoritative source** (basic/derived split eliminated, NFO controls removed). The 97 requirements are drawn from the SP 800-53 Rev. 5 moderate baseline.
- **ODPs:** many requirements contain `[Assignment: organization-defined …]` placeholders. For DoD work the values are **set by DoD's ODP memorandum**, not by you — apply DoD's values, don't pick your own. A Qualified Individual / 3PAO (CMMC C3PAO) signs off on the assessment; cross-border CUI storage and FedRAMP-equivalence of your cloud need separate legal/contract review.

## Requirements
### 03.01 Access Control (AC)
- **Requires:** Limit system access to authorized users, processes, and devices; enforce account management, least privilege, separation of duties, session controls, remote-access and wireless restrictions, and control of CUI flow (03.01.01–03.01.22, e.g. 03.01.01 Account Management, 03.01.02 Access Enforcement, 03.01.05 Least Privilege, 03.01.12 Remote Access).
- **In code:** Postgres **Row-Level Security** for record-level CUI scoping; Supabase Auth roles + RBAC for least privilege and separation of duties; deny-by-default policies; session timeout / re-auth; restrict admin paths behind Cloudflare Access / Zero Trust; isolate CUI into a dedicated tenant/enclave (see Rev. 3 enclave-scoping note below).
- **Primitive:** 5 (Access control — RLS / RBAC / MFA / least-privilege).
- **Cite:** SP 800-171r3 §03.01 (03.01.01–03.01.22).

### 03.02 Awareness and Training (AT)
- **Requires:** Provide security-awareness training (including insider-threat and CUI-handling) to users; train privileged/role-based personnel (03.02.01–03.02.02).
- **In code:** Operational, not codeable — onboarding training records and role-based training for anyone with CUI access; track completion dates in your vendor/personnel register.
- **Primitive:** 4 (Data inventory + sensitive-field tagging) — pairs with knowing who touches tagged CUI.
- **Cite:** SP 800-171r3 §03.02 (03.02.01–03.02.02).

### 03.03 Audit and Accountability (AU)
- **Requires:** Create, protect, and retain system audit logs sufficient to trace actions to individual users; define auditable events; enable review/analysis/reporting; protect logs from tampering; time-stamp and synchronize; restrict audit-management to privileged roles (03.03.01–03.03.08).
- **In code:** Append-only / immutable **audit logging** of auth, CUI access, and admin actions; ship logs off-box (Cloudflare Logpush, Fly log shipping) to write-once storage; NTP-synced timestamps; restrict log access via RLS/RBAC. Shares the encryption-at-rest control with 03.13.
- **Primitive:** 6 (Immutable audit logging).
- **Cite:** SP 800-171r3 §03.03 (03.03.01–03.03.08).

### 03.04 Configuration Management (CM)
- **Requires:** Establish and maintain baseline configurations; enforce security configuration settings; control and log changes; apply least-functionality; maintain a system component inventory; control user-installed software (03.04.01–03.04.12).
- **In code:** Infrastructure-as-code baselines (Fly `fly.toml`, Supabase migrations) under version control; PR-gated change control; **SBOM generation + dependency audit in templates/ci/security.yml** to inventory components; disable unused ports/services/extensions; restrict Postgres extensions and admin tooling.
- **Primitive:** 4 (Data inventory + sensitive-field tagging) and 5 (Access control).
- **Cite:** SP 800-171r3 §03.04 (03.04.01–03.04.12).

### 03.05 Identification and Authentication (IA)
- **Requires:** Uniquely identify and authenticate users, processes, and devices; enforce **multifactor authentication** for network access to privileged and non-privileged accounts; manage authenticators; enforce password/cryptographic-module strength; obscure feedback; **replay-resistant authentication** (03.05.01–03.05.12).
- **In code:** Supabase Auth with **MFA/TOTP** enforced for all CUI-system accounts; unique service identities (no shared creds) for Fly/Supabase/Cloudflare; FIPS-validated authenticators where DoD ODP requires; replay-resistant tokens (OIDC/PKCE).
- **Primitive:** 5 (Access control — RLS / RBAC / MFA / least-privilege).
- **Cite:** SP 800-171r3 §03.05 (03.05.01–03.05.12).

### 03.06 Incident Response (IR)
- **Requires:** Establish an incident-handling capability (preparation, detection/analysis, containment, recovery); test it; track/document/report incidents to designated internal and external authorities (03.06.01–03.06.05).
- **In code:** Documented IR runbook + **breach pipeline** wired to alerting; the **DFARS 7012 72-hour DoD cyber-incident report** to dibnet.dod.mil is a hard external obligation here; preserve images/logs for forensic submission for **at least 90 days from submission of the cyber incident report** (DFARS 252.204-7012(e)). Shares the breach-pipeline control with GDPR Art. 33–34 / HIPAA Breach Notification — build once.
- **Primitive:** 8 (Incident-response + breach pipeline).
- **Cite:** SP 800-171r3 §03.06 (03.06.01–03.06.05); reporting obligation per DFARS 252.204-7012(c).

### 03.07 Maintenance (MA)
- **Requires:** Perform and control system maintenance; control maintenance tools, personnel, and remote/nonlocal maintenance; sanitize equipment removed for off-site maintenance (03.07.01–03.07.06).
- **In code:** On managed infra most physical maintenance is the provider's responsibility (inherited control — document the inheritance). Control your nonlocal admin access (Cloudflare Access + MFA), and sanitize any CUI before decommissioning volumes/snapshots.
- **Primitive:** 5 (Access control) and 7 (Retention + deletion jobs) for sanitization.
- **Cite:** SP 800-171r3 §03.07 (03.07.01–03.07.06).

### 03.08 Media Protection (MP)
- **Requires:** Protect (physical and digital) media containing CUI; restrict access; sanitize or destroy media before disposal/reuse; mark media; control transport; encrypt CUI on portable storage (03.08.01–03.08.09).
- **In code:** **AES-256 encryption at rest** on Supabase/Postgres volumes and Fly volumes; cryptographic erase / sanitization jobs on deletion; no CUI on un-encrypted portable media; mark/tag CUI datasets in the data inventory.
- **Primitive:** 1 (Encryption — FIPS-validated where required), 4 (Data inventory + tagging), 7 (Retention + deletion jobs).
- **Cite:** SP 800-171r3 §03.08 (03.08.01–03.08.09).

### 03.09 Personnel Security (PS)
- **Requires:** Screen individuals before authorizing CUI access; protect CUI during personnel actions such as transfers and terminations (revoke access, recover assets) (03.09.01–03.09.02).
- **In code:** Joiner/mover/leaver process that revokes Supabase/Fly/Cloudflare credentials on termination; tie access provisioning to screening status in the personnel register.
- **Primitive:** 5 (Access control) and 9 (Vendor / sub-processor register) for personnel records.
- **Cite:** SP 800-171r3 §03.09 (03.09.01–03.09.02).

### 03.10 Physical Protection (PE)
- **Requires:** Limit physical access to systems and facilities; escort visitors; maintain physical-access logs; control physical access to output devices; protect alternate work sites (03.10.01–03.10.08).
- **In code:** Largely **inherited from the managed-infra provider** (Fly/Supabase/Cloudflare data-center physical security) — document the inheritance and retain the provider's SOC 2 / FedRAMP attestation. Your residual scope: physical security of any local workstation that touches CUI.
- **Primitive:** 9 (Vendor / sub-processor register) — capture inherited controls via provider attestations.
- **Cite:** SP 800-171r3 §03.10 (03.10.01–03.10.08).

### 03.11 Risk Assessment (RA)
- **Requires:** Periodically assess risk to operations and CUI; scan for and remediate vulnerabilities; respond to findings; update risk assessments (03.11.01–03.11.04, including vulnerability monitoring/scanning).
- **In code:** **Dependency-audit + gitleaks secret-scanning in templates/ci/security.yml** on every PR; scheduled vulnerability scans of Fly machines and dependencies; documented remediation SLAs tied to severity.
- **Primitive:** 8 (Incident-response + breach pipeline) for remediation tracking; supports 4 (Data inventory).
- **Cite:** SP 800-171r3 §03.11 (03.11.01–03.11.04).

### 03.12 Security Assessment and Monitoring (CA)
- **Requires:** Assess security requirements; develop and maintain a **plan of action and milestones (POA&M)**; monitor controls on an ongoing basis; maintain a **system security plan (SSP)** describing system boundary, environment, and how requirements are met (03.12.01–03.12.05).
- **In code:** Maintain the SSP and POA&M as living documents; assess against **SP 800-171A Rev. 3** (422 determination statements) — this is the basis of the CMMC assessment and the SPRS score. Continuous monitoring via your audit-log and alerting pipeline.
- **Primitive:** 6 (Immutable audit logging) and 9 (Vendor / sub-processor register) for documenting inherited controls in the SSP.
- **Cite:** SP 800-171r3 §03.12 (03.12.01–03.12.05); assessment per SP 800-171A Rev. 3.

### 03.13 System and Communications Protection (SC)
- **Requires:** Monitor and protect communications at system boundaries; deny-by-default / allow-by-exception network traffic; separate user and management functionality; prevent unauthorized information transfer; **implement cryptography to protect CUI confidentiality** (03.13.11) and protect CUI in transit and at rest; manage cryptographic keys; use FIPS-validated cryptography (03.13.01–03.13.15).
- **In code:** **TLS 1.2+** everywhere (Cloudflare edge, Fly, Supabase); boundary protection / WAF at Cloudflare; **AES-256 at rest**; separate management plane from the application plane. **Rev. 3 nuance:** 03.13.11 takes an `[Assignment: organization-defined types of cryptography]` ODP — DoD's ODP requires **FIPS-validated (FIPS 140) cryptography** for CUI, so plain TLS/AES is insufficient unless the module is FIPS-validated; verify your provider's crypto module validation. Shares the encryption control with HIPAA §164.312(a)(2)(iv)/(e) and GDPR Art. 32 — but FIPS validation is the stricter bar here.
- **Primitive:** 1 (Encryption — **FIPS-validated**, TLS 1.2+ in transit, AES-256 at rest, key management).
- **Cite:** SP 800-171r3 §03.13 (03.13.01–03.13.15; numbering is non-contiguous — 03.13.16 exists but is Withdrawn, so 03.13.15 Session Authenticity is the highest active requirement; cryptographic protection 03.13.11).

### 03.14 System and Information Integrity (SI)
- **Requires:** Identify, report, and correct system flaws in a timely manner; protect against malicious code; monitor system security alerts/advisories and act; perform system monitoring to detect attacks/indicators; manage and retain CUI within the system per applicable requirements (03.14.01–03.14.08, with 03.14.04, 03.14.05, 03.14.07 Withdrawn; including 03.14.08 Information Management and Retention).
- **In code:** Automated patch/flaw remediation pipeline; dependency-audit in security CI feeds flaw identification; malicious-code protection at the edge (Cloudflare) and in the build pipeline; alerting on security advisories; runtime monitoring feeding the incident pipeline; information management and retention of CUI tied to your retention/deletion jobs (03.14.08).
- **Primitive:** 8 (Incident-response + breach pipeline); supports 6 (Immutable audit logging) and 7 (Retention + deletion jobs) for 03.14.08.
- **Cite:** SP 800-171r3 §03.14 (03.14.01–03.14.08; 03.14.04, 03.14.05, 03.14.07 Withdrawn; 03.14.08 Information Management and Retention).

### 03.15 Planning (PL) — *Rev. 3 addition*
- **Requires:** Develop and maintain a system security plan; define and enforce **rules of behavior** for users with CUI access (03.15.01–03.15.03).
- **In code:** SSP (shared with 03.12) documents architecture, boundary, and control implementation; published acceptable-use / rules-of-behavior acknowledged by all CUI users.
- **Primitive:** 4 (Data inventory + sensitive-field tagging) — the SSP anchors the CUI boundary and tagging.
- **Cite:** SP 800-171r3 §03.15 (03.15.01–03.15.03).

### 03.16 System and Services Acquisition (SA) — *Rev. 3 addition*
- **Requires:** Apply security engineering principles; address security in the system development life cycle; define and enforce external-system-service requirements (03.16.01–03.16.03).
- **In code:** Secure-by-design SDLC with security CI gates (SBOM, gitleaks, dep-audit) baked into templates/ci/security.yml; flow security requirements down to any external service you build on; document service-provider obligations in contracts.
- **Primitive:** 9 (Vendor / sub-processor register) and supports 1/5/6 via secure SDLC.
- **Cite:** SP 800-171r3 §03.16 (03.16.01–03.16.03).

### 03.17 Supply Chain Risk Management (SCRM) — *Rev. 3 addition*
- **Requires:** Develop and implement a supply-chain risk management plan and processes; apply SCRM controls to acquired system components and services; protect against supply-chain risks (03.17.01–03.17.03).
- **In code:** **SBOM in templates/ci/security.yml** as the component-provenance backbone; vendor/sub-processor register capturing Supabase, Fly, Cloudflare and their security attestations + flow-down terms; pin and verify dependencies. Shares the SBOM control with EU CRA and NIS2 supply-chain obligations — build the SBOM once, map to all three.
- **Primitive:** 9 (Vendor / sub-processor register — BAA / DPA / security addendum) and 4 (Data inventory).
- **Cite:** SP 800-171r3 §03.17 (03.17.01–03.17.03).

## Evidence to retain
- **System Security Plan (SSP)** describing the CUI boundary/enclave, environment of operation, inherited (provider) controls, and how each of the 97 requirements is met (03.12 / 03.15).
- **Plan of Action and Milestones (POA&M)** for any unimplemented or partially-implemented requirements, with target completion dates.
- **SP 800-171A Rev. 3 assessment results** (the 422 determination statements) and the **SPRS self-assessment score** (DFARS 252.204-7019/-7020); for CMMC Level 2, the **C3PAO certification** record.
- **Applied DoD ODP values** — evidence that organization-defined parameters use DoD's published values, not self-selected ones (notably FIPS-validated cryptography for 03.13.11).
- **FIPS 140 validation evidence** for the cryptographic modules protecting CUI (CMVP certificate numbers from your providers).
- **Audit logs** retained per the DoD ODP retention period for 03.03 (apply the contract-specified value); breach/incident reports submitted to DoD within 72 hours under DFARS 7012, with preserved images and malware samples retained for **at least 90 days from submission of the cyber incident report** (DFARS 252.204-7012(e)).
- **Configuration baselines, change records, and component inventory / SBOM** (03.04, 03.17).
- **Training completion records, personnel screening, and joiner/mover/leaver access-revocation logs** (03.02, 03.09).
- **Vendor / sub-processor register** with provider security attestations (SOC 2 / FedRAMP), flow-down clauses, and documentation of inherited physical/maintenance controls (03.07, 03.10, 03.16, 03.17).
- **Risk assessments and vulnerability-scan / remediation records** (03.11, 03.14).
- A **Qualified Individual / DPO-equivalent or C3PAO** sign-off on the SSP, ODP application, and FIPS-validation claims before submitting a SPRS score or entering a CMMC assessment — these carry False Claims Act exposure if misrepresented.
