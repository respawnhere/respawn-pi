# Compliance reference documentation — sources & licenses

Official regulatory and standards texts behind the [requirements checklists](../requirements/). The freely-redistributable ones are retained as PDFs in the RespawnPack pack (this `references/` folder), downloaded **2026-06-20** (default-scope frameworks) and **2026-06-22** (conditional/sector frameworks); the table below gives each one's source link + license. Copyrighted standards, statutes with no clean single-file official PDF, and live-amended regulations are **linked, not vendored**. (The pack ships the checklists + this citation list into installed repos; the PDFs themselves stay in the RespawnPack repo — download from the links below.)

These are unmodified official texts retained for convenience. They are **not legal advice** and may be superseded — verify the current version at the source. For EU texts, only the version published in the Official Journal is authentic.

## Linked only (copyrighted, or no clean single-file official download)
- **SOC 2 — Trust Services Criteria** — © AICPA. Obtain from [aicpa-cima.com](https://www.aicpa-cima.com/resources/landing/system-and-organization-controls-soc-suite-of-services). The [`soc2.md`](../requirements/soc2.md) checklist enumerates the CC1–CC9 structure from public summaries, **not** the protected criteria text.
- **PCI-DSS v4.0.1** — © PCI Security Standards Council. Free download (with terms acceptance) at the [PCI SSC document library](https://www.pcisecuritystandards.org/document_library/). Reference-by-link.
- **ISO/IEC 27001:2022** — © ISO/IEC; sold by ISO, not redistributable. [iso.org/standard/27001](https://www.iso.org/standard/27001). Reference-by-link.
- **CCPA / CPRA** — Cal. Civil Code §1798.100–1798.199 (public domain), but no clean single-file official PDF. Statute at [leginfo.legislature.ca.gov](https://leginfo.legislature.ca.gov/faces/codes_displayText.xhtml?division=3.&part=4.&lawCode=CIV&title=1.81.5); regulations at [cppa.ca.gov](https://cppa.ca.gov/regulations/).
- **US state privacy laws** (VCDPA, Colorado CPA, Connecticut CTDPA, Utah UCPA, Texas TDPSA, …) — public-domain statutes, linked per state in [`us-state-privacy.md`](../requirements/us-state-privacy.md).
- **GLBA Safeguards Rule (16 CFR Part 314) + Privacy Rule (16 CFR Part 313)** — US federal regulation, public domain, but the current amended text (incl. the §314.4(j) breach-notification rule effective May 13 2024) is cleanest at the live eCFR, not a static annual edition. [ecfr.gov Part 314](https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-16/chapter-I/subchapter-C/part-314) · FTC [Safeguards guidance](https://www.ftc.gov/business-guidance/resources/ftc-safeguards-rule-what-your-business-needs-know). The Privacy Rule is now largely operative as CFPB Reg P (12 CFR 1016). Reference-by-link.
- **COPPA Rule (16 CFR Part 312)** — US federal regulation, public domain; the 2025 amended rule (effective June 23 2025, compliance April 22 2026) is at the live eCFR plus the Federal Register final rule. [ecfr.gov Part 312](https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-16/chapter-I/subchapter-C/part-312) · [2025 final rule](https://www.federalregister.gov/documents/2025/04/22/2025-05904/childrens-online-privacy-protection-rule). Reference-by-link.
- **Sarbanes-Oxley Act (SOX)** — US statute, public domain; the relevant obligations span the Act (§302 = 15 U.S.C. §7241, §404 = §7262, §802 = 18 U.S.C. §1519, §906 = §1350) plus **PCAOB AS 2201** and the **COSO** framework — no single official compliance PDF. Sources linked per requirement in [`sox.md`](../requirements/sox.md).
- **PIPEDA** (S.C. 2000, c. 5) — Canadian federal statute; © Crown, reproducible under the Reproduction of Federal Law Order, but the consolidation is served as HTML, not a clean single-file PDF. Statute at [laws-lois.justice.gc.ca](https://laws-lois.justice.gc.ca/eng/acts/P-8.6/); OPC guidance at [priv.gc.ca](https://www.priv.gc.ca/en/privacy-topics/privacy-laws-in-canada/the-personal-information-protection-and-electronic-documents-act-pipeda/). Reference-by-link.
- **Quebec Law 25** — the consolidated *Act respecting the protection of personal information in the private sector* (CQLR c. P-39.1, as amended by 2021 c. 25) is served as HTML by LégisQuébec; no clean single official PDF. [legisquebec.gouv.qc.ca P-39.1](https://www.legisquebec.gouv.qc.ca/en/document/cs/P-39.1); CAI guidance at [cai.gouv.qc.ca](https://www.cai.gouv.qc.ca/protection-renseignements-personnels/sujets-et-domaines-dinteret/principaux-changements-loi-25). Reference-by-link.
